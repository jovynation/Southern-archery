Ready

Before opening day

Five minutes at home saves a wasted morning in the stand.

  1. Buy your license and tag before the season, not the morning of.
  2. Sight in with the slugs or ammo you'll actually hunt with.
  3. Shoot your bow with your hunting broadheads, not just field points.
  4. Check the DNR's blaze orange rules for your season.
  5. Pack a knife, a drag rope, and something warm.

Missing something? Call (712) 262-7213 before you drive over.

FFL

FFL transfers

Bought it online? Ship it here and pick it up in Spencer.

  1. Pick your gun from any online seller.
  2. Call the shop and ask them to send their license to the seller.
  3. The seller ships to the shop, and the shop calls you when it lands.
  4. Come in with a valid photo ID showing your current address.
  5. Fill out ATF Form 4473 while the shop runs the federal background check.

Federal law: 18 or older for rifles and shotguns, 21 or older for handguns from a dealer. Call for the current transfer fee.

Over 40 years on 11th Street

How a Spencer archery shop became the gun and bow store hunters know today, and what customers say about it.

1982

Our story

Four decades of bows, guns, and hunting talk on 11th Street.

1982

Southern Archery opens as an archery and hunting accessories store. When the Spencer Daily Reporter visited in 2014, it had been in business 32 years.

1986

The shop settles at 305 11th Street SE for 28 years. Owner Ted Bruning's furniture and antiques hobby grows big enough to share the floor with the bows.

2004

Local bowhunters on the Iowa Whitetail forum are already recommending the staff.

2014

Ted acts as his own general contractor on a new building at 325 11th Street SW, next to Cindy's Steakhouse. More visible, more secure, room to grow. The furniture goes; the hunting gear stays. Read the 2014 story.

Today

A licensed firearms dealer and gunsmith, a Henry Gold Dealer, and Spencer's stop for guns, bows, Ravin crossbows, ammo, and Browning safes.
